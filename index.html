IDENTIFICATION ENGINE

HOW BIG MATCHES & MUST-WINS ARE IDENTIFIED

1. Tournament Knockouts & Elimination Stakes (1.50x Multiplier)

Every delivery is tagged by match stage metadata. Matches qualify for the 1.50x Big-Match Knockout Multiplier if knock_out == 1 OR stage is classified as Final, Eliminator, Qualifier 1, Qualifier 2, Semi Final, or Super 4. Defeat terminates the campaign.

2. The 'Crucible Match Win' Attribution Filter (Authentic MWPPs)

Eliminates empty stat-padding by requiring all 4 criteria simultaneously:

  • Outcome Mandate: Team MUST have won the match (team_won == 1). Runs or wickets in lost causes receive zero match-win credit.
  • Minimum Leverage Threshold: High-Pressure WPA ≥ +0.50 (or HP strike rate ≥ 160 with ≥ 20 runs scored under pressure).
  • Crisis / Finishing Requirement: Either Death Chase WPA ≥ 1.0 or Crisis Rescue WPA ≥ 1.5.

3. Schedule Strength & Opposition Tiering

  • Tier 1 Elite (1.25x Boost): All IPL franchise clashes, India, Australia, England, South Africa, New Zealand, Pakistan, West Indies.
  • Associate / Minnow (0.40x Discount): Bilateral series against lower-tier associates (e.g. Zimbabwe, UAE, Netherlands, Oman) on low-par pitches.
DYNAMIC PRESSURE ENGINE

DYNAMIC BALL-BY-BALL PRESSURE DETECTION

1. Instantaneous Leverage Index (LI ≥ 1.50)

Evaluated on every delivery as the derivative of Win Probability: LI = (ΔWP_wicket - ΔWP_boundary) / σ_WP. Isolates deliveries where a single boundary or wicket swings match probability by at least 1.5x the historical volatility (top 15% game-defining deliveries).

2. Scoreboard Chase Pressure (RRR ≥ 10.0 RPO)

Triggered in second innings run chases where required run rate reaches or exceeds 10.0 runs per over with fewer than 8 wickets in hand. Evaluates ice-cold boundary hitting under escalating scoreboard pressure.

3. Crisis Collapse Windows (Early & Middle Overs)

Identifies deliveries where the batting team is reeling in a top-order collapse: Powerplay Crisis (Overs 1–6 with ≥ 2 wickets lost) or Middle Overs Collapse (Overs 7–12 with ≥ 4 wickets lost). Distinguishes authentic innings resurrection from front-running.

4. Death Crucible (Overs 16–20)

Deliveries in the final four overs when match margin is under 36 runs with result undecided. Death yorkers and boundary balls carry maximal leverage multipliers.

THE XGBOOST ML CONTEXT ENGINE

Rather than using crude batting averages or economy rates, our model evaluates performance against ball-by-ball expected runs E[Runs] trained on 190,811 deliveries using 18 predictive contextual features:

  • Ground & Venue Par: Historical ground scoring index and pitch pacing rate.
  • Innings Phase: Overs remaining and exact ball number in the current over.
  • Wickets & Pressure: Cumulative wickets lost and current match run rate.
  • Target Dynamics: Required run rate and target differential in second-innings chases.
  • Tactical Matchups: Batter career archetype vs bowler arm and skill (RHB vs SLA, LHB vs Off-Spin).
SAMPLE RELIABILITY FILTER:

To prevent single-match flukes, scores are scaled by a square-root reliability curve: Reliability = sqrt(Matches / 45) capped at 1.0. Batters must also satisfy the Substantive Volume Standard (≥ 500 balls faced) to eliminate cameo specialists.
